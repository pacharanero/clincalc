# Clinical Calculator Roadmap

The clinical-calculator backlog, grouped strictly by completion status. Calculator categorisation by specialty / setting / status lives in [tags](../docs/calculators.md#filtering-by-tag) on each calculator - this file is purely a list of what is being built and what is queued.

**Engineering, infrastructure, GUI, distribution, and any other non-calculator work lives in [`spec/roadmap.md`](roadmap.md), not this file.** Keeping the two split means a clinician scanning the calculator backlog is not buried in build-tooling items, and an engineer scanning the build-tooling roadmap is not buried in clinical scores.

Roadmap items have stable identifiers so they can be referred to in conversation, commits, PRs, and release notes. Do not renumber existing IDs just because items are completed or removed.

Completed calculators are removed from this file rather than kept as roadmap history. The shipped catalogue is [`docs/calculators.md`](../docs/calculators.md).

MedikQuantis parity items follow the normal calculator completion rule: an independently implemented calculation is complete when it passes the primary-source, licence, closed-schema, clinical-safety, and testing gates. Translation availability is tracked independently in the README calculator-status table and does not block calculator completion. A locale is advertised only after its complete attributed bundle passes the review gates in [`multilingual.md`](multilingual.md) and [`docs/translating.md`](../docs/translating.md). Upstream MedikQuantis logic and tests are useful cross-project evidence, not a substitute for clincalc's primary-source verification.

## Status legend

- `[~]` **In-progress** - actively being implemented or under review.
- `[ ]` **Planned** - committed to build; the next batch.
- Items under [Future](#future) are explicitly **under consideration** rather than committed - they get promoted to **Planned** when scheduled.

### Planned

Sourced from a 2026-10-16 cross-reference of MDCalc's public tool catalogue against clincalc's registry, looking for clinically significant gaps with a clean primary-literature formula or decision rule (not MDCalc's own copyrighted presentation). All 20 are classic public-domain-formula scores from the original peer-reviewed publication, the same licensing tier as the great majority of already-shipped calculators; none carry a known rights restriction.

- [ ] **CALC-070 Rockall Score (upper GI bleeding)** - Pre-endoscopy and full (post-endoscopy) mortality/rebleeding risk after acute upper GI haemorrhage. Pairs with the shipped `glasgow_blatchford` (pre-endoscopy triage) by covering the post-endoscopy risk-stratification step. Rockall TA, Logan RF, Devlin HB, Northfield TC. Risk assessment after acute upper gastrointestinal haemorrhage. Gut. 1996;38(3):316-321.
- [ ] **CALC-071 Ranson's Criteria (acute pancreatitis severity)** - 11-parameter severity/mortality score for acute pancreatitis, scored at admission and 48 hours. Ranson JH, Rifkind KM, Roses DF, Fink SD, Eng K, Localio SA. Prognostic signs and the role of operative management in acute pancreatitis. Surg Gynecol Obstet. 1974;139(1):69-81.
- [ ] **CALC-072 Glasgow-Imrie Criteria (acute pancreatitis severity)** - UK-preferred 8-parameter alternative to Ranson's, scored at 48 hours. Blamey SL, Imrie CW, O'Neill J, Gilmour WH, Carter DC. Prognostic factors in acute pancreatitis. Gut. 1984;25(12):1340-1346.
- [ ] **CALC-073 BISAP Score (acute pancreatitis severity)** - Simplified 5-point early (within 24h) mortality-risk score for acute pancreatitis, usable before 48-hour labs are available. Wu BU, Johannes RS, Sun X, Tabak Y, Conwell DL, Banks PA. The early prediction of mortality in acute pancreatitis: a large population-based study. Gut. 2008;57(12):1698-1703.
- [ ] **CALC-074 Modified Rankin Scale (post-stroke disability)** - Standard 0-6 ordinal global-disability scale after stroke; pairs with the shipped `nihss`/`abcd2`. Rankin J. Cerebral vascular accidents in patients over the age of 60. Scott Med J. 1957;2(5):200-215 (original 5-level scale); van Swieten JC, Koudstaal PJ, Visser MC, Schouten HJ, van Gijn J. Interobserver agreement for the assessment of handicap in stroke patients. Stroke. 1988;19(5):604-607 (current 0-6 modified version).
- [ ] **CALC-075 ROSIER Scale (stroke recognition)** - UK-developed emergency-room stroke-recognition instrument (NICE NG128-aligned ambulance/ED use). Nor AM, Davis J, Sen B, et al. The Recognition of Stroke in the Emergency Room (ROSIER) scale: development and validation of a stroke recognition instrument. Lancet Neurol. 2005;4(11):727-734.
- [ ] **CALC-076 Ottawa Ankle Rule** - Decision rule for selective ankle/midfoot radiography after acute ankle injury. Stiell IG, McKnight RD, Greenberg GH, et al. Implementation of the Ottawa ankle rules. JAMA. 1994;271(11):827-832.
- [ ] **CALC-077 Ottawa Knee Rule** - Decision rule for selective knee radiography after acute knee injury. Stiell IG, Wells GA, Hoag RH, et al. Implementation of the Ottawa Knee Rule for the use of radiography in acute knee injuries. JAMA. 1997;278(23):2075-2079.
- [ ] **CALC-078 Canadian C-Spine Rule** - Decision rule for selective cervical-spine imaging after blunt trauma in alert, stable patients. Stiell IG, Wells GA, Vandemheen KL, et al. The Canadian C-Spine Rule for radiography in alert and stable trauma patients. JAMA. 2001;286(15):1841-1848.
- [ ] **CALC-079 NEXUS Criteria (cervical-spine imaging)** - Alternative US-derived low-risk criteria for selective c-spine imaging; commonly presented alongside the Canadian C-Spine Rule. Hoffman JR, Mower WR, Wolfson AB, Todd KH, Zucker MI. Validity of a set of clinical criteria to rule out injury to the cervical spine in patients with blunt trauma. National Emergency X-Radiography Utilization Study Group. N Engl J Med. 2000;343(2):94-99.
- [ ] **CALC-080 Shock Index (adult and paediatric age-adjusted)** - HR/SBP ratio for early haemorrhagic/sepsis shock recognition, with the paediatric age-adjusted (SIPA) threshold variant. Allgöwer M, Burri C. Schockindex. Dtsch Med Wochenschr. 1967;92(43):1947-1950 (adult); Acker SN, Ross JT, Partrick DA, Tong S, Bensard DD. Pediatric specific shock index accurately identifies severely injured children. J Pediatr Surg. 2015;50(2):331-334 (SIPA).
- [ ] **CALC-081 Light's Criteria (pleural effusion)** - Exudate-versus-transudate classification from pleural-fluid-to-serum protein and LDH ratios. Light RW, Macgregor MI, Luchsinger PC, Ball WC Jr. Pleural effusions: the diagnostic separation of transudates and exudates. Ann Intern Med. 1972;77(4):507-513.
- [ ] **CALC-082 Winters' Formula (metabolic acidosis compensation)** - Expected respiratory (PaCO2) compensation for a primary metabolic acidosis; pairs with the shipped `anion_gap`. Albert MS, Dell RB, Winters RW. Quantitative displacement of acid-base equilibrium in metabolic acidosis. Ann Intern Med. 1967;66(2):312-322.
- [ ] **CALC-083 Serum Osmolality / Osmolar Gap** - Calculated serum osmolality and the osmolar gap against a measured value, for toxic-alcohol and unexplained-osmolar-gap workup; pairs with the shipped `corrected_sodium`/`free_water_deficit` cluster. Standard calculated-osmolality formula (2 x Na + glucose/18 + urea/2.8 in US units, or 2 x Na + glucose + urea in SI units); osmolar-gap interpretation per Glasser L, Sternglanz PD, Combie J, Robinson A. Serum osmolality and its applicability to drug overdose. Am J Clin Pathol. 1973;60(5):695-699.
- [ ] **CALC-084 Parkland Formula (burns fluid resuscitation)** - First-24-hour crystalloid resuscitation volume from weight and %TBSA burned. Baxter CR, Shires T. Physiological response to crystalloid resuscitation of severe burns. Ann N Y Acad Sci. 1968;150(3):874-894.
- [ ] **CALC-085 PESI / simplified PESI (sPESI)** - 30-day mortality risk once pulmonary embolism is diagnosed, complementing the shipped pre-test `wells_pe` score; both the original and simplified variants in one calculator. Aujesky D, Obrosky DS, Stone RA, et al. Derivation and validation of a prognostic model for pulmonary embolism. Am J Respir Crit Care Med. 2005;172(8):1041-1046; Jiménez D, Aujesky D, Moores L, et al. Simplification of the pulmonary embolism severity index for prognostication in patients with acute symptomatic pulmonary embolism. Arch Intern Med. 2010;170(15):1383-1389.
- [ ] **CALC-086 Canadian Syncope Risk Score** - 30-day risk of serious adverse events after ED assessment of syncope; chosen over the older San Francisco Syncope Rule as the more recent and more extensively validated instrument. Thiruganasambandamoorthy V, Kwong K, Wells GA, et al. Development of the Canadian Syncope Risk Score to predict serious adverse events after emergency department assessment of syncope. CMAJ. 2016;188(12):E289-E298.
- [ ] **CALC-087 Truelove-Witts Severity Index (ulcerative colitis)** - Classic mild/moderate/severe UC flare classification from stool frequency, blood, and systemic features, guiding the need for hospitalisation and IV therapy. Truelove SC, Witts LJ. Cortisone in ulcerative colitis; final report on a therapeutic trial. Br Med J. 1955;2(4947):1041-1048.
- [ ] **CALC-088 Mallampati Classification (airway assessment)** - Ordinal oropharyngeal-view classification predicting difficult laryngoscopy, same classification-only pattern as the shipped `nyha`/`ehra`/`mrc_dyspnoea`. Mallampati SR, Gatt SP, Gugino LD, et al. A clinical sign to predict difficult tracheal intubation: a prospective study. Can Anaesth Soc J. 1985;32(4):429-434.
- [ ] **CALC-089 Kocher Criteria (paediatric septic arthritis)** - Four-criteria clinical prediction rule distinguishing septic arthritis from transient synovitis of the hip in children. Kocher MS, Zurakowski D, Kasser JR. Differentiating between septic arthritis and transient synovitis of the hip in children: an evidence-based clinical prediction algorithm. J Bone Joint Surg Am. 1999;81(12):1662-1670.

### Future

Calculators worth shipping, under consideration. Largely surfaced from sibling open-source projects (notably [MedikQuantis](https://medikquantis.me), MIT licensed). Clinical context for each lives in the [docs catalogue wishlist](../docs/calculators.md#wishlist-candidates-for-future-addition).

#### MedikQuantis parity gaps

This is the remaining calculation gap against MedikQuantis's live 65-calculator registry at upstream commit [`16c63c85`](https://github.com/laurapiro17/medikquantis/tree/16c63c85aee7a64417205f60cb3e66fccf19fae2), reviewed 2026-08-29. clincalc ships 61 equivalent calculations and lacks the 4 below. MedikQuantis's README still reports 49 calculators, so its registry is the authoritative inventory. Review this snapshot when upstream changes; add new stable `CALC-*` items rather than silently letting parity drift.

For every item below, completion means independent implementation from the primary source; closed schema, licence evidence, and literature-vector tests under the normal calculator contract; and explicit comparison with the pinned MedikQuantis behavior with intentional clinical or guideline differences documented. Machine identifiers and numeric behavior remain locale-neutral. Reviewed translations are tracked independently in the README and do not block completion.

The upstream combined BMI / BSA / ideal-body-weight item (`CALC-056`) is completed through the existing standalone BMI and Mosteller BSA calculators plus `CALC-066`, the named NIH-NHLBI ARDSNet adult predicted-body-weight protocol. This intentionally does not reproduce the upstream generic Devine/adjusted-weight outputs: the upstream panel applies adult BMI to paediatric-sized inputs, treats a dosing scalar as a personal ideal weight, and applies an uncited adjusted-weight formula whenever actual weight exceeds rounded Devine weight. Adjusted weight is indication- and protocol-specific, so no universal value is emitted.

`CALC-027` ORBIT is completed as an independent implementation of the factual five-factor method and observed rates in O'Brien et al. The source article is CC BY-NC 4.0, so clincalc does not reproduce its prose, tables, figures, or presentation. The legal provenance is the expression-method distinction in [WIPO Copyright Treaty Article 2](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/295166): copyright protection extends to expression, not ideas, procedures, methods of operation, or mathematical concepts as such. Unlike the pinned upstream calculator, which accepts pre-resolved anaemia and renal booleans and labels category incidence as annual risk percent, clincalc requires raw sex-branch haemoglobin, haematocrit, and eGFR values and reports score-specific and category-level observed bleeds per 100 patient-years, explicitly not a personalised annual probability. Its broad numeric upper bounds are clincalc input-safety guards against unit and data-entry errors, not source scoring thresholds.

`CALC-026` NYHA is completed from the NCI Thesaurus Class I-IV definitions, which NCI distributes under CC BY 4.0. clincalc requires explicit attestation of defined or presumed cardiac disease and a mutually exclusive functional profile, then returns the ordinal and Roman class without the pinned upstream calculator's unsupported treatment recommendations. The response records the selected profile and NCI concept identifier and warns that NYHA is subjective, particularly across Classes II and III: Raphael et al. reported only 54% interobserver concordance.

- [ ] **CALC-034 SCORAD** - Atopic dermatitis severity; blocked pending explicit unrestricted permission or legal review because [ePROVIDE identifies SCORAD as all rights reserved](https://eprovide.mapi-trust.org/instruments/scoring-in-atopic-dermatitis) and the [Eczema Foundation legal notice](https://www.pierrefabreeczemafoundation.org/en/legal-notice-po-scorad) reserves adaptation, translation, and software-integration rights

#### QResearch / ClinRisk candidates

Further QResearch-derived algorithms from the same ClinRisk open-source lineage as the shipped `qrisk3`, `qfracture`, and `qdiabetes` ports. ClinRisk also mirrors source for `qKidney`, `qStroke`, `qThrombosis`, `qbleed`, and `qStatin` at [nhsland/clinrisk-modules](https://github.com/nhsland/clinrisk-modules) under the same README-stated LGPL/AGPL licensing rationale; none of those has had its individual `COPYING` file verified yet, so none has a `CALC-*` identifier until that licensing scan happens.

- [ ] **CALC-069 QCancer** - Sex-specific absolute risk across roughly ten cancer sites (blood, breast, cervical, colorectal, gastro-oesophageal, lung, ovarian, pancreatic, renal tract, uterine for women; an analogous male-specific set) from routinely-collected primary-care risk factors and new symptoms, informing NICE NG12 suspected-cancer referral thresholds. ClinRisk released the source under AGPL-3.0 at [nhsland/clinrisk-modules](https://github.com/nhsland/clinrisk-modules/tree/master/qCancer), same faithful-reimplementation rationale as QRISK3/QDiabetes. Two blockers before implementation: (1) the openly-released coefficients are QCancer-2013 vintage (source files dated 2012-13); qcancer.org documents a 2017 recalibration and Hippisley-Cox has published further recalibration work since (CanPredict, 2023/2025), and no newer open coefficient release has been confirmed - do not present 2013 coefficients as the current algorithm without disclosing the vintage explicitly, and do not implement at all until a current open release is confirmed or the vintage disclosure is accepted as sufficient; (2) QCancer is not one score but roughly ten independent per-site logistic sub-models per sex, so a single-calculator-returning-a-vector-of-risks vs. several separate `CALC-*` entries decision is needed before implementation starts.

#### ADHD screening / diagnostic tools (rights-review)

Beyond the shipped `asrs` (the ASRS-v1.1 adult screener, with an explicit NYU/Harvard free-with-attribution grant), checked 2026-10-16 for any other ADHD rating scale with a comparably clean licence. None found one; all three below are blocked pending an explicit rights-holder grant or legal review, not asserted as prohibited.

- [ ] **CALC-090 Vanderbilt ADHD Diagnostic Rating Scales (parent/teacher, children 6-12)** - Blocked pending explicit permission or legal review: NICHQ's own page offers only the 2002 first edition free with an attribution request, not an unrestricted reproduction/reimplementation grant comparable to EPDS's; co-authored with McNeil (a pharmaceutical company), and the current third edition is sold via the AAP Bookstore.
- [ ] **CALC-091 SNAP-IV (Swanson, Nolan and Pelham rating scale)** - Blocked pending explicit permission or legal review: distributor statements conflict - one states "printed with permission, all rights reserved. copies may be made by..." while another secondary source claims public domain; the restrictive statement is more consistent with the scale's actual distribution and has not been resolved by a primary-source grant.
- [ ] **CALC-092 Wender Utah Rating Scale (WURS)** - Blocked pending explicit permission or legal review: no unrestricted-reproduction statement found in the 1993 primary paper (Ward, Wender, Reimherr, Am J Psychiatry), and it is tracked by MAPI Trust's ePROVIDE, which generally indicates active rights management even where clinical use is nominally free.

Conners' Rating Scales / CAARS / CAADID are not tracked here: MHS's own manuals explicitly prohibit reproduction, adaptation, or redistribution without written permission, the same confirmed-proprietary tier as `cat`/`acq`/`mmse`. DIVA-5, the structured interview most widely used in UK adult ADHD pathways, was also checked: the non-profit DIVA Foundation charges a per-clinician fee (not free) and the instrument itself is "reprinted with permission" from DSM-5, adding a second rights dependency (APA) beyond DIVA's own terms - also not a clean grant, and not added here only because it was not among the three scales the user asked to track.

#### Other candidates

Shipped anthropometric and body-composition measures include BMI, body-fat circumference, Jackson-Pollock skinfolds, BAI, WHtR, WHR, RFM, FFMI, SMI, 1RM, training zones, and the Wilks score. Remaining non-MedikQuantis candidates include other lab or field methods for body composition and the alternative DOTS strength score.

- [ ] **CALC-036 StatinMD** (Oxford STRATIFY) - personalised 1/5/10-year risk of serious statin-induced muscle disorders; natural pairing with QRISK3 (benefit vs harm). Academic licence via Oxford University Innovation (Cai et al, *Lancet Digital Health* 2026; [licence page](https://process.innovation.ox.ac.uk/software/p/25396/stratify---stainmd-risk-calculator---academic-use/1))
- [ ] **CALC-032 RCPCH Digital Growth Charts** - UK-WHO + UK90; z-score / centile / SDS, chart rendering. Needs LMS tables (binary-size variable) and confirmation of RCPCH licensing.
- [ ] **CALC-044 Energy-restricted protein / macronutrient target** - protein or broader macronutrient targets from weight or lean body mass and a defined goal, including lean-mass retention during a caloric deficit. The shipped `protein_target` calculator deliberately does not complete this candidate: Morton et al. excluded energy-restricted participants and supports only a total-body-weight protein amount for healthy adults undertaking resistance training.
- [ ] **CALC-046 DOTS** - alternative bodyweight-adjusted strength score to the shipped Wilks calculator; needs DOTS's polynomial coefficients confirmed against a citable primary source or official federation technical documentation, not just secondary calculator sites, before implementation
- [ ] **CALC-053 Axial length centile charts (CREAM-Kids)** - age-, sex-, and region-specific centile charts for axial eye length in children and adolescents, from the CREAM-Kids Consortium (Kneepkens, Lingham, Mackey et al, *JAMA Ophthalmology* 2026). Reusable coefficients or tables and their distribution terms must be confirmed before implementation. Do not infer a serial rate-of-change risk model unless a primary source specifies and validates one. Shared centile-engine work is tracked only under ENG-010 in [`roadmap.md`](roadmap.md#eng-010-generic-centile-engine).
